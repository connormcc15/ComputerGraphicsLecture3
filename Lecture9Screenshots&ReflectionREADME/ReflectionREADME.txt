1. Glass and Material Vertex Fragment
I made the glass shader. Originally, I couldn't achieve the glass look however, it seemed to be a common issue. I saw the posed fix for it and implemented it into the scene.
Glass shader can and will be use for anything that is glass in a video game, whether its a window, glass cup, etc.
The shader can also be for anything you want to look glass like but is not exactly glass.

2. Water and Scrolling Water
I created the water shader that was able to have different wave lengths, frequency and speeds. I also created the water scrolling shader where the water can move either on the x and/or y axis.
The regular water shader can be used for water within a game whether its an actual body of water or maybe even a drink by just changing some of the parameters within the shader.
For the scrolling water, that can be used for waterfalls or fast moving parts of water in a level or world.

3. Simple Lighting with Shadows and Simple Shadows with Texture
I created the both the simple lighting shadow and simple shadow with texture.
This shaders can be used to help with lighting in a dark space or even to make a game more realistic or scary.

I wish I could have play around with the shaders a bit more than I usually do but overall, I generally understand where all these shaders can be used.
